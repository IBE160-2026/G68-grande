## Leads
- Trial length conflict: 7 days vs reportedly 14 days for quarterly.
- Help center "four priorities" lists five → stale docs.
- Unclear whether cover-letter scan has its own score.
- Billing/cancellation friction strongest complaint pattern.
- Learnable: missing-keyword list, accept/reject per suggestion with live score, concrete target (75%), ATS explainer.
